Privacy Policy - CookMate AI

Last updated: July 2026

1. Information We Collect

CookMate AI collects the following information:

  • Email address and name (for login)
  • Recipe preferences and history
  • Ingredient scan images (processed by AI, not stored)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide personalized recipe recommendations
  • To save your meal plans and favorites
  • To improve our AI recommendations

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In for authentication
  • Supabase for data storage
  • Google AdMob for advertisements
  • Groq AI for recipe generation
  • Google Gemini for image scanning

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information. Your data is stored securely on Supabase servers.

5. Cookies

CookMate AI uses local storage to save your preferences and login information on your device.

6. Children's Privacy

CookMate AI is not intended for children under 13 years of age.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: cookmate.ai.app@gmail.com

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
